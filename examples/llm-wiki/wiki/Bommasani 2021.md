# On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models

## Summary
Coins the term foundation model for models trained on broad data at scale and adapted to many downstream tasks, and surveys their opportunities and risks.

## Explanation
The report identifies two defining properties: emergence, where capabilities arise implicitly from scale, and homogenization, where one model becomes the base for many applications so that its defects are inherited downstream.

It is explicitly sociotechnical, spanning capabilities, technical principles, applications such as law, healthcare and education, and societal impact such as inequity, misuse and environmental cost.
